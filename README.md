# THE WORLD — Friday 20 November 2026

A one-page newspaper front page summing up the world's news of
**Friday 20 November 2026**, for the make.fun bounty
`news-image-20261120` ($250, top 50 paid).

## What the image shows

- **Masthead** — "Friday 20 November 2026" with the day's sub-headline:
  Cyclone Akvan, Kyiv missiles, COP31 final day, Asia sell-off.
- **Figures strip** — six numbers from the day: 298,676 evacuated ahead
  of Cyclone Akvan; 27 ballistic missiles among 110+ fired at Kyiv;
  −6.63% Taiex Friday close; 2.3% Japan October CPI; 118, Glenn
  Phillips' century vs India; 8 years, BepiColombo's trip to Mercury.

- **Ten feature tiles** — Asia's market crash; BepiColombo on the eve

- **Also-list** — 27 more Friday stories, from UK retail sales and the

Every story on the page happened, or was first reported, on Friday
20 November 2026 in at least one time zone. Claims are attributed,
not stated as fact.

## How to run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python standard library only, no network) copies
`entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `out`),
validating it is a PNG of 1024×1024 under 1 MiB, and prints its size.

```sh
python3 main.py --self-test
```

runs the copy twice into temporary directories and confirms the two
outputs are byte-identical.
